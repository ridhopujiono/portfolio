## Overview

The School Attendance System aims to streamline student tracking by linking physical biometric hardware to a digital database, incorporating an automated notification workflow.

## Technical Approach

- **Biometric Integration**: Designed to receive attendance logs from physical fingerprint scanners.
- **Data Management**: Uses a local MySQL database for record-keeping and synchronization flows.
- **Notifications**: Incorporates automated WhatsApp messaging workflows to alert stakeholders of attendance events.
